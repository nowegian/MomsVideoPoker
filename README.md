# Mom's Video Poker

A casino-style video poker game that runs in any web browser — no install, no server.

**Play it:** https://nowegian.github.io/MomsVideoPoker/

## Games
- **Double Double Bonus** – big bonuses for four Aces / four 2s–4s, extra with the right kicker
- **Bonus Poker** – higher pays for four Aces and four 2s–4s
- **Jacks or Better** – the classic

25¢ per credit, bet 1–5 credits ($1.25 max). Pair of Jacks or better wins.

## Features
- Classic double-headed court cards — winning Jacks, Queens and Kings wink at you
- Strategy toggle that highlights the best cards to hold (computed from the pay table)
- Stats panel: hands played, net, payback %, biggest win, hand frequencies, "played by the book" %
- Add $100 of play credits any time
- Special sounds for straights, flushes and full houses; fanfare + confetti for four of a kind or better

Play money only. Credits and stats are saved in your browser.

## Keyboard
`Space` deal/draw · `1–5` hold · `B` bet one · `M` bet max · `S` strategy
